# Master Design Intelligence & Archetype Handbook

This handbook defines the 12 primary visual design archetypes used by `document-maker` to guarantee distinct, publication-grade document styling. Every generated document adopts a coherent visual identity instead of default or AI-templated appearances.

---

## 1. The 12 Visual Design Archetypes

### 1. Modern Corporate (`modern_corporate`)
- **Ideal For**: Executive summaries, quarterly business reviews, management presentations, RFP proposals.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Navy (`#0A192F` / `RGB(10, 25, 47)`)
  - Secondary: Steel Blue (`#1E3A8A` / `RGB(30, 58, 138)`)
  - Accent: Electric Sky (`#38BDF8` / `RGB(56, 189, 248)`)
  - Canvas: Off-White / Ghost Slate (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Card Fill: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Typography**: Header: Inter / Arial Bold; Body: Calibri / Helvetica (10.5–11pt), 1.25x line spacing.
- **Components**: Crisp 1pt card borders (`#E2E8F0`), subtle drop-shadows, high-contrast KPI cards.

### 2. Minimalist Editorial (`minimalist_editorial`)
- **Ideal For**: Whitepapers, thought leadership monographs, strategic position papers, architectural essays.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Ink Black (`#111827`)
  - Secondary: Charcoal Slate (`#374151`)
  - Accent: Warm Terracotta (`#C2410C`)
  - Canvas: Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`)
  - Divider: Soft Muted Grey (`#E5E7EB`)
- **Typography**: Header: Merriweather / Georgia; Body: Georgia / Garamond (11pt), generous 1.35x line spacing.
- **Components**: 1.25-inch outer margins, elegant 0.5pt horizontal hairline dividers, pull quotes with oversized quotation marks.

### 3. Academic & Scientific (`academic_scientific`)
- **Ideal For**: Journal submissions, lab records, thesis chapters, technical benchmark reports, symposium papers.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Midnight / Monochromatic (`#000000` text on `#FFFFFF`)
  - Accent: Oxford Royal (`#1E293B`)
  - Code BG: Soft Zinc (`#F4F4F5`)
- **Typography**: Header: Times New Roman / Garamond Bold; Body: Times New Roman (10pt or 11pt), justified paragraphs, 1.15x spacing.
- **Components**: Strict numbered headings (1.0, 1.1, 1.1.1), table captions positioned *above* tables, figure captions *below* figures, formal IEEE/APA citation blocks.

### 4. Premium Business & Executive (`premium_business`)
- **Ideal For**: Board of directors decks, investor pitch presentations, private equity memos.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Forest Green (`#064E3B`)
  - Secondary: Rich Emerald (`#047857`)
  - Accent: Warm Champagne Gold (`#D97706`)
  - Canvas: Pale Mint Mist (`#F0FDF4`)
- **Typography**: Header: Montserrat / Trebuchet MS Bold; Body: Aptos / Calibri (11pt).
- **Components**: Bento-box cards, oversized metric badges (36–48pt), luxury metallic accent dividers.

### 5. Technology & Futuristic (`technology_futuristic`)
- **Ideal For**: Cloud architecture specifications, cybersecurity audits, API references, DevOps runbooks.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Obsidian Slate (`#0B0F19`)
  - Secondary: Deep Indigo (`#1E293B`)
  - Accent: Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`)
  - Highlight: Cyber Violet (`#8B5CF6`)
  - Canvas: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`) for slides / Crisp Code Grey (`#F1F5F9`) for documents
- **Typography**: Header: Trebuchet MS / Fira Code; Code: JetBrains Mono / Consolas; Body: Inter / Segoe UI.
- **Components**: High-contrast syntax code blocks, containerized pipeline diagrams, badge pills with micro-borders.

### 6. Creative & Experimental (`creative_experimental`)
- **Ideal For**: Creative agency pitches, product launches, portfolio showcases, design retrospectives.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Violet (`#581C87`)
  - Secondary: Vibrant Tangerine (`#EA580C`)
  - Accent: Electric Amber (`#F59E0B`)
  - Canvas: Sand / Warm Alabaster (`#FEF3C7` / `#FAFAF9`)
- **Typography**: Header: Heavy Impact / Sans ExtraBold; Body: Clean Modern Sans (11pt).
- **Components**: Asymmetrical containers, dynamic color blocking, diagonal accent stripes.

### 7. Government & Institutional (`government_institutional`)
- **Ideal For**: Public policy briefs, regulatory filings, compliance audits, NGO statements.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Federal Navy (`#1E3A5F`)
  - Secondary: Crimson Accent (`#991B1B`)
  - Neutral: Parchment Slate (`#F1F5F9`)
- **Typography**: Header: Book Antiqua / Georgia; Body: Arial / Calibri.
- **Components**: WCAG AAA high-contrast accessibility compliance, numbered clauses, formal signature & stamp seals.

### 8. Engineering & Technical (`engineering_technical`)
- **Ideal For**: Industrial SOPs, mechanical & hardware datasheets, civil engineering specifications.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Industrial Blueprint Blue (`#0284C7`)
  - Secondary: Charcoal Steel (`#334155`)
  - Accent: Safety Amber (`#F59E0B`)
  - Canvas: Crisp Engineering Paper (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Typography**: Header: DIN Alternate / Arial Bold; Body: Consolas / Tahoma.
- **Components**: High data density, structured parameter matrices, numbered execution steps, schematics.

### 9. Educational & Instructional (`educational_instructional`)
- **Ideal For**: Student laboratory records, workshop workbooks, training manuals, examination papers.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Academic Royal Blue (`#2563EB`)
  - Secondary: Forest Pine (`#059669`)
  - Accent: Sunflower (`#F59E0B`)
  - Canvas: Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) with soft tint cards (`#F0FDF4`)
- **Typography**: Header: Trebuchet MS Bold; Body: Calibri / Arial (11pt).
- **Components**: Step badges, callout boxes for "Key Takeaways" and "Common Pitfalls", structured code-and-output pairings.

### 10. Magazine & Publication (`magazine_publication`)
- **Ideal For**: Annual reports, industry newsletters, public affairs reviews.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Onyx (`#18181B`)
  - Secondary: Crimson Red (`#DC2626`)
  - Canvas: White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Typography**: Header: Playfair Display / Georgia; Body: Calibri / Garamond.
- **Components**: Drop caps, 2-column or 3-column layouts, full-width breakout quotes, sidebars.

### 11. Luxury & Hospitality (`luxury_hospitality`)
- **Ideal For**: High-end real estate dossiers, luxury brand proposals, bespoke hospitality guides.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Deep Espresso (`#271810`)
  - Secondary: Warm Bronze (`#92400E`)
  - Accent: Soft Champagne (`#EAB308`)
  - Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FFFFF0`)
- **Typography**: Header: Bodoni / Didot / Georgia; Body: Light Sans / Garamond.
- **Components**: Generous whitespace, refined 0.5pt borders, understated gold badges.

### 12. Data-Driven Analytical (`data_analytical`)
- **Ideal For**: Financial models, telemetry dashboards, BI reports, econometric audits.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Slate Black (`#0F172A`)
  - Series Colors: Blue (`#2563EB`), Emerald (`#10B981`), Amber (`#F59E0B`), Coral (`#EF4444`)
  - Canvas: Data Sheet Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Typography**: Header: Inter Bold; Data/Numbers: Consolas / Roboto Mono; Body: Inter.
- **Components**: Zebra striping (`#F1F5F9`), numeric right-alignment, delta indicators ($\Delta$), freeze panes.

## 2. Universal Design Tokens & Rules

| Token | Specification | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Page Geometry** | A4 (`595.28 x 841.89 pt`) or Letter (`612 x 792 pt`) | Standard international vs. US print |
| **Margins** | Default: `54 pt` (0.75 in) or `72 pt` (1.0 in) | Symmetrical, never $\le 36 pt$ |
| **Presentation Canvas** | Native 16:9 (`13.333 x 7.5 in` / `960 x 540 pt`) | Standard modern widescreen projection |
| **Body Line Spacing** | 1.15x to 1.35x (14–16pt leading on 11pt font) | Ensures optical breathing room |
| **Paragraph Gap** | 6pt after paragraphs; 16–20pt before major headings | Eliminates cluttered density |
| **Contrast Ratio** | $\ge 4.5:1$ for normal text, $\ge 3:1$ for large headings | WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility mandatory |
| **Table Header Fill** | Dark primary archetype color with bold white text | High contrast data distinction |
| **Table Alternate Row** | 3–5% tint of slate or canvas (`#F8FAFC`) | Prevents horizontal scanning fatigue |
