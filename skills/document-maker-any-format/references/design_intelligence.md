# Design Intelligence & Archetype Handbook

This guide details the 12 primary visual design archetypes used by `document-maker-any-format`.

---

## 1. The 12 Design Archetypes

### 1. Modern Corporate
- **Best for**: Quarterly business reviews, strategic plans, consulting deliverables, enterprise proposals.
- **Palette**: Deep Navy (`#0A192F`), Cobalt Blue (`#1E3A8A`), Cool Slate (`#64748B`), Accent Sky (`#38BDF8`), Card Canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Typography**: Header: Inter / Arial Bold; Body: Calibri / Helvetica (10.5–11pt), 1.2x line spacing.
- **Card Styles**: 1pt subtle border (`#E2E8F0`), 8pt rounded corners (in HTML/presentations), clean white containers.

### 2. Minimalist Editorial
- **Best for**: Whitepapers, architectural briefs, opinion essays, think-tank monographs.
- **Palette**: Ink Black (`#111827`), Warm Charcoal (`#374151`), Pale Cream (`#FDFBF7`), Accent Terracotta (`#C2410C`).
- **Typography**: Header: Georgia / Merriweather; Body: Garamond / Georgia (11pt), generous 1.35x line spacing.
- **Layout Rules**: 1.25-inch outer margins, thin horizontal rules (`#E5E7EB`), pull quotes with italicized serif styling.

### 3. Academic & Scientific
- **Best for**: Research papers, technical journals, university project reports, dissertations.
- **Palette**: Monochromatic (`#000000` text on `#FFFFFF`), Steel Navy (`#1E293B`) section accents.
- **Typography**: Times New Roman / Computer Modern serif (10pt or 11pt), justified paragraphs, 1.15x spacing.
- **Layout Rules**: Formal numbered section headers (1.0, 1.1, 1.1.1), centered tables with uppercase table captions *above*, figure captions *below*.

### 4. Premium Business & Executive
- **Best for**: Boardroom presentations, private equity memos, investor pitch decks.
- **Palette**: Deep Forest Green (`#064E3B`), Champagne Gold (`#D97706`), Midnight (`#09090B`), Crisp Card (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography**: Header: Montserrat / Trebuchet MS; Body: Aptos / Calibri (11pt).
- **Layout Rules**: Bento-box cards, oversized metric badges (36–48pt), high contrast stat pills.

### 5. Technology & Futuristic
- **Best for**: System architecture documents, API specifications, DevOps roadmaps, cybersecurity audits.
- **Palette**: Dark Slate (`#0B0F19`), Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`), Neon Purple (`#A855F7`), Crisp Light Grey (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Typography**: Header: Fira Code / Trebuchet MS; Code: Consolas / Courier New; Body: Segoe UI / Inter.
- **Layout Rules**: Monospace parameters, syntax-highlighted code blocks, pipeline architecture diagrams.

### 6. Creative & Experimental
- **Best for**: Brand books, creative agency pitches, portfolio showcases, design retrospectives.
- **Palette**: Tangerine (`#EA580C`), Rich Plum (`#581C87`), Warm Sand (`#FEF3C7`), Off-Black (`#18181B`).
- **Typography**: Header: Heavy sans-serif; Body: Clean neutral sans (11pt).
- **Layout Rules**: Dynamic asymmetrical containers, diagonal accents, vibrant breakout cards.

### 7. Government & Institutional
- **Best for**: Policy briefs, compliance audits, public notices, NGO whitepapers.
- **Palette**: Federal Navy (`#1E3A5F`), Crimson Accent (`#991B1B`), Parchment Grey (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Typography**: Header: Georgia / Book Antiqua; Body: Arial / Calibri.
- **Layout Rules**: High contrast compliance (WCAG AAA), clear numbered paragraphs, explicit appendix hierarchy.

### 8. Engineering & Technical
- **Best for**: Hardware specifications, industrial manuals, standard operating procedures (SOPs).
- **Palette**: Blueprint Blue (`#0284C7`), Industrial Charcoal (`#334155`), Safety Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **Typography**: Header: Arial / DIN Alternate; Body: Consolas / Tahoma.
- **Layout Rules**: High data density, structured parameter tables, step-by-step schematics.

### 9. Educational & Instructional
- **Best for**: Student manuals, lab records, training handbooks, study guides.
- **Palette**: Academic Royal (`#2563EB`), Emerald Green (`#059669`), Soft Background (`#F0FDF4`).
- **Typography**: Header: Trebuchet MS; Body: Calibri / Arial (11pt).
- **Layout Rules**: Highlighted concept boxes, step numbers in circles, key takeaway banners.

### 10. Magazine & Publication
- **Best for**: Company newsletters, annual reports, industry magazines.
- **Palette**: Bold Magenta (`#BE185D`), Rich Black (`#0F172A`), Pale Lavender (`#FDF4FF`).
- **Typography**: Header: Playfair Display / Georgia; Body: Open Sans / Calibri.
- **Layout Rules**: Multi-column text, large decorative pull quotes, full-width photo breakouts.

### 11. Luxury & Hospitality
- **Best for**: Real estate brochures, luxury hospitality proposals, high-end retail catalogs.
- **Palette**: Deep Bronze (`#78350F`), Warm Gold (`#B45309`), Warm Ivory (`#FFFBEB`).
- **Typography**: Header: Didot / Bodoni / Garamond; Body: Century Gothic / Calibri.
- **Layout Rules**: Generous white space, hairline borders (0.5pt), delicate typography tracking.

### 12. Data-Driven Analytical
- **Best for**: Financial statements, market research, econometric models, analytics dashboards.
- **Palette**: Tableau Blue (`#1F77B4`), Muted Orange (`#FF7F0E`), Forest (`#2CA02C`), Grid Grey (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Typography**: Header: Arial Bold; Body: Calibri (10pt numbers aligned tabular).
- **Layout Rules**: Right-aligned numeric data, integrated delta indicators, summary KPI cards.

## 2. Typography Rules & Hierarchy Scale

| Level | Size | Weight | Line Spacing | Keep With Next | Space Before / After |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Title** | 26–32pt | Bold | 1.1x | Yes | 18pt / 12pt |
| **Subtitle** | 14–16pt | Regular / Italic | 1.2x | Yes | 0pt / 24pt |
| **Heading 1** | 18–20pt | Bold | 1.15x | Yes | 18pt / 6pt |
| **Heading 2** | 14–16pt | Bold | 1.15x | Yes | 12pt / 4pt |
| **Heading 3** | 12–13pt | Semi-bold | 1.2x | Yes | 8pt / 2pt |
| **Body Text** | 10.5–11pt | Regular | 1.2–1.3x | No | 0pt / 6pt |
| **Table Cells** | 9–10pt | Regular | 1.1x | No | 2pt / 2pt |
| **Captions / Footnotes** | 8.5–9pt | Italic | 1.1x | No | 4pt / 4pt |
